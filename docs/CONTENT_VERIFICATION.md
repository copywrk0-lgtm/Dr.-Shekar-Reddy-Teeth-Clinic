# Clinic content verification sheet

Owner approval required for:

- Clinic name
- Primary phone and WhatsApp
- Landline
- Address and landmark
- Daily hours
- Holiday hours
- Doctor names, degrees and registrations
- Treatment/service list
- Rating wording and review-source links
- Review excerpts and attribution
- Clinic photographs
- Any claims about pricing, sittings, pain, follow-up or same-day care

Do not publish an unverified claim simply because it appears in an old directory or draft.
